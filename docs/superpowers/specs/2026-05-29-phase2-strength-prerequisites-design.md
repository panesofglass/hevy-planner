## Context

The user has completed Phase 1 (Joint Restoration, 8 weeks). Most Phase 1 gate tests are passing; two gaps remain: strict pull-ups (close but not at 8) and single-leg balance (eyes closed, 30s). Phase 2 builds strength prerequisites toward 5 skill goals over 12 weeks (weeks 9–20 of the 12-month roadmap).

Key constraints:
- Equipment: pull-up bar with dip arms, SelectTech dumbbells to 80 lb, bench, resistance bands
- Session length: 20–30 min, 3–5 days/week
- Scheduling: sequential queue rotation — no day-of-week locking; "what's next" regardless of day
- Exercise philosophy: maximum muscle coverage per movement (evidence-based compound selection)
- Hypertrophy goal: chest and arms specifically — user is experiencing mass loss

## Exercise Selection Rationale

Exercises were selected using a "maximum muscle coverage per movement" criterion backed by EMG research:

- **Pull-ups + chin-ups** (alternating): pull-up emphasizes lats; chin-up has significantly higher bicep activation. Together they cover the full vertical pull pattern
- **Dumbbell row:** horizontal pull — covers mid-back and rear delts not reached by vertical pull alone
- **ATG split squat + RDL:** EMG-confirmed to cover the entire lower body in two movements (ATG = anterior chain; RDL = posterior chain)
- **Dips:** research confirms the most comprehensive push exercise (chest, triceps, anterior delts, serratus anterior simultaneously)
- **Incline press + archer push-up + floor press:** three chest angles (upper/mid/lower) for hypertrophy; addresses the mass loss concern
- **Lateral raise + face pull:** all three shoulder heads require distinct exercises; lateral (side delt) is undertrained by all compound movements
- **Suitcase carry:** adds anti-lateral-flexion — the one core training vector not covered by hollow body (anti-extension) or Pallof press (anti-rotation)

Wall sit was considered but research found it primarily activates quads isometrically with minimal hamstring or glute recruitment — not a high-coverage movement.

## Progressions (3 phases, 12 weeks)

### Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
Learn the Phase 2 movement patterns. Establish baseline loads.
- Pull-ups: band-assisted; aim for 5+ unassisted by end of phase
- ATG split squat: bodyweight only, partial depth acceptable
- RDL: 25–35 lb each, focus on hinge pattern
- Dips: band-assisted if needed
- Incline press: 25–35 lb each
- Archer push-up: support hand on fist if needed
- Floor press: 20–30 lb each
- Wall handstand: face-to-wall, hold 10–20s
- Planche lean: 5–10° lean, 5–8s
- L-sit: bent-knee tuck hold, 3–5s
- Hollow body: knees bent, 15–20s
- Suitcase carry: 20–30 lb, 20s each side

### Build (Weeks 5–8)
Increase load and time under tension. Reduce assistance.
- Pull-ups: reduce band by one level; target 8+ unassisted
- False grip hang: add active shoulder depression; 15–20s
- ATG split squat: add 10–15 lb each, full depth
- RDL: 35–50 lb each, 1–2s pause at bottom
- Dips: reduce/eliminate band; 2–3s pause at bottom
- Archer push-up: reduce support, increase lean
- Wall handstand: 30–45s; try chest-to-wall position
- Planche lean: 15–20° lean, 10s
- L-sit: half L-sit (one leg extended), 5–8s
- Hollow body: arms overhead, 20–30s
- Suitcase carry: 30–45 lb, 30s each side

### Integration (Weeks 9–12)
Peak strength expression. Skill emergence begins.
- Pull-ups: 10+ unassisted; first weighted reps (+5–10 lb)
- False grip hang: commit to false grip position; 20–30s
- ATG split squat: 20–25 lb each; approach pistol depth with support hand available
- RDL: 50–65 lb each; introduce single-leg RDL variation
- Dips: bodyweight + attempt +10–25 lb weighted
- Archer push-up: minimal support; approach one-arm negative
- Wall handstand: 45–60s; begin controlled kick-up
- Pike push-up: full handstand push-up negative (lower to head)
- Planche lean: 20–25°, 10–15s; first tuck planche attempts
- L-sit: full L-sit, 5–10s
- Hollow body: full hollow body rocks
- Suitcase carry: 45–55 lb, 30s each side

## New Skill: One-Arm Push-Up

Added to the roadmap's skills array as a 5th skill goal (equal priority).

- **Current state:** Phase 1 built shoulder stability and core. Regular push-ups available.
- **Requirements:** Chest (all angles), tricep mass, core anti-rotation, wrist conditioning, scapular stability
- **Timeline:** First one-arm negative week 20–24; first rep week 32–40; 3–5 reps week 48+
- **Milestones:**
  1. 15 strict push-ups (clean form) — Week 10
  2. Close-grip push-ups 10 reps — Week 14
  3. Archer push-ups 5 each side — Week 20
  4. One-arm negative (3s descent) — Week 28
  5. First one-arm push-up — Week 36
  6. 3–5 consecutive — Week 48

## Exercise Archiving Policy

Exercises must never be deleted from program JSON files — only archived by setting `"archived": true` on the exerciseTemplate. This preserves historic tracking across phases.

Implementation requires adding an optional `archived: boolean` field to `$defs.exerciseTemplate` in `schema/program.schema.json` before writing the new program.
